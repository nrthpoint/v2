Burbeck

We collaborated with BSTN LDN to deliver a new digital presence for luxury property company Burbeck. BSTN LDN had been providing a complete rebrand for the company, and part of that was a fresh, updated online home to showcase their latest projects and offerings.

We launched the site on Squarespace, allowing us total freedom regarding the front-end experience and a seamless, intuitive experience for the content editors managing the site. Our project setup followed the usual process for a Squarespace template: a local environment setup with Squarespace Toolbelt and Webpack, Sass and Babel to utilise the latest front-end standings, then build a bundle ready for upload to Squarespace.

BSTN LDN designed this site with a mobile-first approach, and our development followed suit. One notable challenge with this project was the varied and complex content that needed to be presented, such as slideshows with nested home data and links. We got to test the limit of Squarespace's custom content modules and try to make them easy to edit and maintain.
