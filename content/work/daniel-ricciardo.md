We partnered with [BSTN LDN](https://www.bastianldn.com) to launch a new home for F1 legend Daniel Ricciardo. The site features the latest news on The Honey Badger's career and insights into the latest antics and merch from streetwear label Enchanté.

To provide an exceptional experience for the end user and the team maintaining the site, we decided to launch it on Squarespace. The build is a fully responsive, mobile-first solution, utilising Sqaurespace's Toolbelt & SASS to deliver a template that utilises the latest front-end standards, building to a template ready for use on Squarespace.

We also provided training and education in the handover to the DR's team, ensuring they were comfortable managing the site independently and that it met all their expectations.
