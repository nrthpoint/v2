We collaborated with Big DB to deliver a complete, multinational relaunch for Eversys, the world-renowned leader in innovative espresso machine solutions.

The site was launched in four different languages simultaneously. It features all the latest information on their current product range as well as news and information on Eversys. Behind the scenes, the site is powered by [Prismic](https://prismic.io), with the front-end built using React, [Gatsby](https://www.gatsbyjs.com), and [Styled Components](https://styled-components.com).
