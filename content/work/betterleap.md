We partnered with **Kind.dev** and **Betterleap** to revolutionise their healthcare recruitment platform with cutting-edge, real-time collaboration tools.

North Point engineered a robust real-time chat system using **WebSockets** and **NestJS** on **Google Cloud Platform**, enabling seamless communication for users. To further enhance the platform, we developed a **Jobs Board**, with bespoke tools for both recruiters and candidates to find and fill open positions. This was developed with a focus on SEO and performance, scoring full marks on Google's Lighthouse.

Additionally, we contributed to the development and launch of their **mobile app**, providing a sleek and intuitive experience for users on the go. This was build using React Native and Expo, where we helped implement chat, jobs and other functionality.
